Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Catando ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Catando app for iOS collects, why, and what choices you have. Catando has no account and no login — we never ask for your name, email, or any personal profile to play the game.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Catando does not require registration, does not collect your name or contact details, and does not store your puzzle progress on any server we control — your progress and badges are saved locally on your device.

2. Information Collected Automatically

Like most apps, Catando relies on a small number of third-party services that may collect limited technical data automatically to make the app work and to keep it free:

ServicePurposeData involved
Advertising partner(s) Show ads that keep the app free to play Device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data
Apple App Store Process the optional "Remove Ads" purchase Purchase confirmation (payment is handled entirely by Apple; the product and price may also appear in the analytics data described in Section 3)
Firebase Analytics (Google) Understand which features are used so we can fix bugs and improve the game Anonymous usage events, standard automatic events (first open, sessions, screen views), in-app purchase details, an anonymous per-installation identifier, device model, OS and app version, approximate region (see Section 3)

We don't control these third parties' own data practices beyond what's needed to run the app; please refer to Apple's privacy documentation and, where applicable, your device's ad-tracking settings to manage this data.

3. Analytics

Catando uses Firebase Analytics, a service from Google, to count how the app is used. The events we record are anonymous: a puzzle was started or completed (with the board size, difficulty and whether it was the daily or a practice puzzle, plus, when completed, whether a hint was used and a rough time range), a hint was used, the War Board was opened or left, a share button was tapped (and which kind of share), an interstitial or rewarded ad was shown, and a Remove Ads purchase or restore happened (which plan, and whether ads are now removed). The events carry no name, email, account or other personal details, and we do not set a user ID.

Firebase Analytics also records its own standard events and technical data automatically: first open, session start, screen views, time in the app, app and operating system updates, and, where the SDK collects them, details of in-app purchases made through Apple (such as the product and price, including Looks, look packs and subscriptions), together with an anonymous identifier for this installation of the app, the device model, the operating system and app version, and an approximate region derived from the network connection. We use the version of Firebase Analytics that does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms; see Google's Firebase privacy information. Analytics data is kept according to our Firebase project's retention settings and Google's policies.

Analytics is started together with advertising: it stays off until the consent form (shown in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) and the iOS tracking prompt have been answered, whichever answer you give to the tracking prompt, because Firebase Analytics as we use it does not use the advertising identifier. Once you have removed ads, analytics is not started on later launches. See Section 5 for how to withdraw consent.

4. How We Use Data

  • To display ads that keep Catando free.
  • To process the optional Remove Ads purchase through Apple.
  • To fix bugs and understand which features people actually use.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to running and improving the App.

5. Your Choices

  • You can limit ad tracking at any time in your device's Settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or your device's ad personalization controls).
  • Purchasing Remove Ads stops in-app ad requests going forward.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Options. If your choices mean that ads can no longer be requested, analytics is switched off as well and the anonymous analytics identifier for your installation is reset. Elsewhere there is currently no separate in-app switch for analytics; deleting the App stops it, and you can contact us with any question or request.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored progress from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Catando is suitable for a general audience and contains no gambling, betting, or real-money wagering mechanics of any kind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll address it.

7. Data Retention & Security

Because Catando doesn't operate user accounts, most of your data (puzzle progress, settings) never leaves your device. Any technical data shared with the third-party services listed above is retained according to those providers' own policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
