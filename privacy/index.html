Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Catando ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Catando app for iOS collects, why, and what choices you have. Catando has no account and no login of its own — we never ask for your email or any personal profile to play the game. The app takes part in the War Board automatically, which uses an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your name or email (see Section 4).

1. Information We Don't Collect

Catando does not require registration, does not collect your name or contact details, and does not store your puzzle progress on any server we control — your progress, settings and badges are saved locally on your device. Apart from the optional display name (Section 4), the only game data that leaves your device is the anonymous analytics events (Section 3) and the team and ranking activity described in Sections 4 and 5.

3. Analytics

Catando uses Firebase Analytics, a service from Google, to count how the app is used. The events we record are anonymous: a puzzle was started or completed (with the board size, difficulty and whether it was the daily or a practice puzzle, plus, when completed, whether a hint was used and a rough time range), a hint was used, the War Board was opened or left, a share button was tapped (and which kind of share), an interstitial or rewarded ad was shown, and a Remove Ads purchase or restore happened (which plan, and whether ads are now removed). The events carry no name, email, account or other personal details, and we do not set a user ID.

Firebase Analytics also records its own standard events and technical data automatically: first open, session start, screen views, time in the app, app and operating system updates, and, where the SDK collects them, details of in-app purchases made through Apple (such as the product and price, including Looks, look packs and subscriptions), together with an anonymous identifier for this installation of the app, the device model, the operating system and app version, and an approximate region derived from the network connection. We use the version of Firebase Analytics that does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms; see Google's Firebase privacy information. Analytics data is kept according to our Firebase project's retention settings and Google's policies.

Analytics is started together with advertising: it stays off until the consent form (shown in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) and the iOS tracking prompt have been answered, whichever answer you give to the tracking prompt, because Firebase Analytics as we use it does not use the advertising identifier. Once you have removed ads, analytics is not started on later launches. See Section 8 for how to withdraw consent.

4. War Board and Teams

The War Board is on by default; the app shows a one-time notice about it and there is no separate on/off switch. Each time you open the app it contacts Firebase (Google) to check the War Board configuration. When you choose a team (Cats or Dogs), or open the board, the app signs you in to Firebase anonymously, and your Energy then counts toward your team's global total and your country or area. The anonymous sign-in creates a random identifier; it is not tied to your name, email or Apple ID, and Catando does not offer another way to sign in. Because the app talks to Google's servers, Google receives technical data such as your IP address and a Firebase installation identifier.

What the War Board stores for you:

  • your anonymous identifier and a generated player handle;
  • your team and your country or area, taken from your device's region unless you choose another in the app;
  • the Energy you add each day and your weekly total (your rank is calculated from these);
  • the avatar you pick;
  • a display name, only if you turn on "Show my name on the board" in Profile and the name passes our automatic name filter. Until then no name leaves your device. If you turn the setting off or the name is rejected or hidden, it is not shown. Pick a name that does not identify you; it is visible to other players.

Other players who view the board can see each listed player's handle, avatar, country or area, and weekly Energy, and your name too if you have turned it on.

If you report another player, we store your report (the reported player's identifier and name as shown, your reason, and your own anonymous identifier) for up to 90 days so that we can review it. Each report is also sent to our support mailbox by email through a third-party email service, including the reported player's identifier, handle and your reason (one of a few fixed categories such as offensive name or impersonation). Those emails are kept in the mailbox and with that service, and Settings → Reset all progress cannot remove them; contact us (Section 12) if you want them deleted.

Requests from the app are checked with Firebase App Check (Apple App Attest or DeviceCheck) so that only the genuine app can use the board. This involves Apple-issued attestation tokens sent to Firebase; it does not include your name or contact details.

War Board data is kept only as long as needed: daily and weekly player records expire after 14 and 60 days respectively, your profile expires after 120 days without activity, country totals are kept for up to 400 days, and an anonymous War Board account that has been inactive for 120 days is deleted automatically. Country totals are anonymous sums and are not removed when you delete your data.

5. iCloud (CloudKit)

Catando's team and ranking features use the public database of its iCloud container (Apple CloudKit). This happens automatically: each time you open the app, if you are signed in to iCloud, it uploads a ranking record with your stage reached under a random identifier, whether or not you have chosen a team, and there is no in-app switch for it. Other records (your team's daily Energy, daily solve times, recruit-link redemptions) are uploaded when you earn Energy for your team, solve a daily puzzle where that feature is on, or redeem a recruit link. The records are anonymous: a random identifier for this installation of the app (not your Apple ID), your stage reached, your team and the amount of Energy for a day, daily solve times, and, if you use a recruit link, the link's token and the day it was redeemed. Apple CloudKit also attaches an opaque record-creator identifier from your iCloud account, which we cannot use to learn your name or email. These records are used to compute team totals and percentile rankings and to credit recruit links. The app reads other players' records from the same public database for this purpose, so records, including the random identifier, the creator identifier and the values above, can be read by the app on other devices. Rankings look only at records updated in the last 30 days, but the records themselves have no expiry and are kept until removed. Catando does not use your private iCloud storage.

The app has no in-app control to delete these CloudKit records yet. Resetting your progress gives the app a new random identifier, but Apple's record-creator identifier for your iCloud account stays the same, so earlier and later records can still be linked to each other. Because these records carry no name or email, we may not be able to tell which records are yours; if you want us to try to remove them, contact us (Section 12). Signing out of iCloud on your device stops new uploads from it.

6. Purchases

Catando offers optional purchases through Apple's App Store: Remove Ads (one-time), Remove Ads (Monthly, an auto-renewing subscription), Catando Plus (an auto-renewing subscription that removes ads and unlocks every Look), and individual or pack Looks (art skins). Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions, renewals and refunds; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app checks your purchases with Apple (StoreKit) to unlock what you bought and stores which Looks you own on your device. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app to recover them on a new device. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

7. How We Use Data

  • To display ads that keep Catando free.
  • To process optional purchases and subscriptions through Apple, and to unlock what you bought.
  • To run the War Board and team rankings, and to review reports of misuse.
  • To fix bugs and understand which features people actually use.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to running and improving the App.

8. Your Choices

  • You can limit ad tracking at any time in your device's Settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or your device's ad personalization controls).
  • Purchasing Remove Ads stops in-app ad requests going forward.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Options. If your choices mean that ads can no longer be requested, analytics is switched off as well and the anonymous analytics identifier for your installation is reset. Elsewhere there is currently no separate in-app switch for analytics; deleting the App stops it, and you can contact us with any question or request.
  • Delete your War Board data: in the app, go to Settings → Reset all progress. This clears your progress on the device and also deletes your War Board data and anonymous account on our servers (it is retried automatically if you are offline), except for the report emails and country totals described in Section 4. The app then takes part in the War Board again automatically with a new anonymous identifier. You can also email us (Section 12) to ask for deletion.
  • Hide your name: switch off "Show my name on the board" in Profile at any time.
  • Subscriptions: cancel in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored progress from your device, but it does not by itself delete data already sent to the War Board or iCloud; use one of the options above.

9. Children's Privacy

Catando is suitable for a general audience and contains no gambling, betting, or real-money wagering mechanics of any kind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and Catando has no chat: the only text one player can show another is the optional display name, which is filtered and hidden until the player turns it on. Catando is not directed to children under 13, and we do not run an age check. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll address it.

10. Data Retention & Security

Because Catando doesn't operate user accounts, most of your data (puzzle progress, settings) never leaves your device. War Board data is retained as described in Section 4. Any technical data shared with the third-party services listed above is retained according to those providers' own policies, except for the CloudKit records (Section 5) and the report emails (Section 4), which we keep until removed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
