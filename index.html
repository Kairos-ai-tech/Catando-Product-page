The only rule you need

Keep it balanced, keep it varied.

No move counters, no combo bonuses to chase — just three quiet checks that every finished Catando grid satisfies.

Never three alike

No animal sits three-in-a-row, across or down. Mix it up before you run long.

Follow the hints

= means the pair matches, × means they differ — every hint holds in the finished grid.

That's genuinely it. No timers, no penalties, no made-up combo score — and because every generated puzzle is solvable by pure logic, you'll never have to place a tile on a coin flip. Sir Whiskers only makes this face when you disturb his nap, not when you're stuck.
